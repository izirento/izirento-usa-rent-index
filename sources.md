## Notes on sources

- California. Civil Code sections 1947.12 (rent cap) and 1950.5 (security), current text on leginfo read on the date of check (English). Local rent control and city rules were not checked.
- New York. General Obligations Law 7-108 (non-rent-stabilized units) and Real Property Law 226-c, text on NYS Open Legislation (English). Rent-stabilized and rent-controlled units, New York City rules and the Good Cause Eviction law were not checked.
- Texas. Property Code chapter 92 (Residential Tenancies), text on the Texas Legislature statutes site read on the date of check, Subchapters A and B (English). The security deposit rules in Subchapter C were not read, so no deposit fact is given.
- Florida. Section 83.49 of the 2025 Florida Statutes (security deposits and advance rent) on the Florida Senate site (English). The rest of Chapter 83, including termination notices, was not read.
- Washington. RCW 59.18.700, 59.18.140 and 59.18.280 on the state legislature site (English). The exemptions in RCW 59.18.710 and local rules were not checked.
- Illinois. 735 ILCS 5/9-209 and 765 ILCS 710/1 on the Illinois General Assembly site (English). Chicago and other local ordinances, which add rules, were not checked, and no statewide deposit cap or rent cap was read.
